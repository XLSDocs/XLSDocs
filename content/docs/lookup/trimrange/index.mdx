Remove blank rows and/or columns from the **outer edges** of a
range or array — trimming away leading/trailing blank space the
same way [TRIM](/docs/text/trim) strips leading/trailing spaces
from text, except TRIMRANGE never touches blanks *inside* the data.

## Return value

`range` with its leading and/or trailing fully-blank rows and
columns removed, according to `trim_rows`/`trim_cols`. Blank rows
or columns sitting *inside* the data, surrounded by non-blank cells
on both sides, are never removed.

## Description

TRIMRANGE solves a specific, common annoyance with a range whose
real extent isn't known in advance — a pasted export with a few
blank rows above the header, or trailing blank rows left over from
deleted data. Rather than manually reselecting the exact non-blank
extent every time the source changes, TRIMRANGE trims it
automatically, and (being a live formula, not a one-time cleanup
step) keeps re-trimming as the source range's blank edges change.

<ExcelCode>{`=TRIMRANGE(A1:E10) → trims any fully-blank leading/trailing rows AND columns (the default, trim_rows=3, trim_cols=3)`}</ExcelCode>

<ExcelCode>{`=TRIMRANGE(A1:E10, 1, 0) → trims only leading blank rows, leaves columns and trailing rows untouched`}</ExcelCode>

<Callout type="warn">
  **TRIMRANGE never removes a blank row or column from the middle of
  the data** — only ones touching the outer edge. A blank row
  between two sections of real data stays exactly where it is,
  regardless of `trim_rows`; TRIMRANGE isn't a general "remove every
  blank" tool, just an edge-trimming one.
</Callout>

## Common errors

### Expecting TRIMRANGE to remove interior blank rows too

<ExcelCode>{`TRIMRANGE(A1:E10) → a blank row at A5:E5 (surrounded by data above and below) is left exactly as-is`}</ExcelCode>

For removing blanks anywhere in the data, not just the edges, use
[FILTER](/docs/arrays/filter) with a non-blank condition instead.

### Forgetting trim_rows and trim_cols are independent

<ExcelCode>{`TRIMRANGE(A1:E10, 1, 2) → trims leading rows AND trailing columns — each argument controls its own dimension separately`}</ExcelCode>

`trim_rows` and `trim_cols` don't have to match — one can trim
leading-only while the other trims trailing-only, or not trim its
dimension at all.

## Related functions

- [TRIM](/docs/text/trim) — the text equivalent, stripping leading/
  trailing spaces from a string instead of blank rows/columns from
  a range.
- [FILTER](/docs/arrays/filter) — removes rows anywhere they don't
  match a condition, not just at the edges.
- [EXPAND](/docs/lookup/expand) — the opposite direction, growing a
  range to a larger fixed size rather than trimming it down.
