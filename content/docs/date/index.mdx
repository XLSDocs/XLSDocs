---
title: Date
description: Functions that build, adjust, and measure differences between dates.
---

Date functions build a date from its parts, get the current date,
shift a date forward or back by months, and measure the difference
between two dates — the building blocks behind deadlines, ages,
tenure, and anything else date-math related.
