---
title: WORKDAY examples
description: Worked examples for WORKDAY — a shipping estimate, holiday exclusion, and finding a date before a given date.
---

<FunctionNav />

<ExampleCard number="01" label="Basic — 10 business days from today" formula={`=WORKDAY(TODAY(), 10)`}>
  Finds the date 10 working days from now, skipping weekends.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="02" label="Shipping estimate with holidays" formula={`=WORKDAY(Orders[ShipDate], 5, Holidays[Date])`}>
  Skips both weekends and any date listed in the Holidays column.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="03" label="A date before a given date" formula={`=WORKDAY(A2, -5)`}>
  A negative `days` value counts backward instead of forward.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="04" label="The very next working day" formula={`=WORKDAY(A2, 1)`}>
  Finds the next working day after A2 — useful for "processed the
  next business day" logic.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="05" label="Custom weekend schedule" formula={`=WORKDAY.INTL(A2, 10, 11)`}>
  [WORKDAY.INTL](/docs/date/workday-intl)'s weekend code `11` treats
  only Sunday as the weekend, matching
  [NETWORKDAYS.INTL](/docs/date/networkdays-intl)'s same code.
</ExampleCard>
