Return the ISO 8601 week number for `date` — the international
standard week-numbering system, where week 1 is defined as the week
containing the year's first Thursday.

## Return value

The ISO 8601 week number of the year containing `date`, as a whole
number from `1` to `53`. `ISOWEEKNUM("3/9/2012")` returns `10`.

## Description

ISOWEEKNUM follows the ISO 8601 international standard for week
numbering, used widely outside the U.S. — weeks always start on
Monday, and week 1 of a year is specifically defined as the week
containing that year's first Thursday (equivalently, the week
containing January 4th). This sometimes puts the very first days of
January into the *last* week of the previous year, which is correct
under the standard even though it can look surprising.

<ExcelCode>{`=ISOWEEKNUM("3/9/2012") → 10`}</ExcelCode>

<Callout type="warn">
  **ISOWEEKNUM and WEEKNUM can disagree**, even on the same date.
  WEEKNUM's default settings start the week on Sunday and always
  put January 1st in week 1; ISOWEEKNUM starts weeks on Monday and
  can place early-January dates in the final week of the *prior*
  year. Use ISOWEEKNUM specifically when ISO 8601 compliance
  matters — for European business reporting, for example.
</Callout>

## Common errors

### #VALUE! — a non-date argument

<ExcelCode>{`ISOWEEKNUM("not a date") → #VALUE!`}</ExcelCode>

`date` must be a real date or a date serial number.
