Return which week of the year a date falls in — week 1 through
52 or 53, depending on how the year lines up with its starting day
of the week.

## Return value

The week number of the year containing `serial_number`, as a whole
number. Week 1 is defined as the week containing January 1st.

## Description

WEEKNUM counts weeks from the start of the year, but "week 1" and
where each week boundary falls both depend on `return_type` — with
the default (`1`), weeks run Sunday through Saturday; with `2`,
weeks run Monday through Sunday instead. Since week 1 is always the
week containing January 1st regardless of how many days of that week
actually fall in January, a date near the start of the year can land
in a different week number depending purely on which day the week is
defined to start on.

<Callout type="warn">
  WEEKNUM's numbering doesn't match the international ISO 8601 week
  standard, which defines week 1 by a stricter rule (the week
  containing the year's first Thursday). For ISO-compliant week
  numbers, use `ISOWEEKNUM` instead — it isn't a `return_type` option
  on WEEKNUM, but a separate function entirely.
</Callout>

March 1, 2026 is a Sunday — with weeks starting on Sunday
(`return_type=1`), that Sunday begins a new week (`10`); with weeks
starting on Monday (`return_type=2`), that same Sunday is still
counted as the end of the previous week (`9`).

## Common errors

### Results don't match a system using ISO week numbers

<ExcelCode>{`WEEKNUM(date) ≠ ISOWEEKNUM(date), for many dates near a year boundary`}</ExcelCode>

WEEKNUM and ISOWEEKNUM use genuinely different definitions of week 1
— they aren't just alternate names for the same calculation. Use
ISOWEEKNUM specifically when ISO 8601 week numbering is required.

### Off-by-one results from the wrong return_type

<ExcelCode>{`WEEKNUM(A2, 1) → one higher than WEEKNUM(A2, 2), for dates falling on a Sunday`}</ExcelCode>

Check which day of the week the business or reporting convention
actually starts on — mixing up `return_type` 1 and 2 is the most
common source of a week number that's consistently one off from
what's expected.
