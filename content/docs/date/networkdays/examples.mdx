title: NETWORKDAYS examples
description: Worked examples for NETWORKDAYS — a basic project timeline, holiday exclusion, and combining with WORKDAY.
---

<FunctionNav />

<ExampleCard number="01" label="Basic — business days in a range" formula={`=NETWORKDAYS(A2, B2)`}>
  Counts weekdays between two dates, excluding Saturdays and Sundays.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="02" label="Excluding company holidays" formula={`=NETWORKDAYS(A2, B2, Holidays[Date])`}>
  Any date in the Holidays column that falls within the range is
  subtracted from the count too.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="03" label="Estimating a delivery deadline" formula={`=NETWORKDAYS(TODAY(), B2)`}>
  Counts how many business days remain between today and a target
  delivery date.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="04" label="Project duration excluding weekends" formula={`=NETWORKDAYS(Project[Start], Project[End]) - 1`}>
  Subtracting 1 converts the inclusive day count into a "days of
  work between the two dates" figure, if the start day itself
  shouldn't count as a full day of work.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="05" label="Custom weekend schedule" formula={`=NETWORKDAYS.INTL(A2, B2, 11)`}>
  [NETWORKDAYS.INTL](/docs/date/networkdays-intl)'s weekend code
  `11` treats only Sunday as the weekend — useful for a business that
  works six days a week.
</ExampleCard>
