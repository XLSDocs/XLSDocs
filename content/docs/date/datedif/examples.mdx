title: DATEDIF examples
description: Worked examples for DATEDIF — age calculation, employee tenure, project duration, and combining units for a full breakdown.
---

<FunctionNav />

<ExampleCard number="01" label="Basic — age in complete years" formula={`=DATEDIF(A2, TODAY(), "Y")`}>
  The standard age formula — complete years between a birth date and
  today.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="02" label="Employee tenure in years and months" formula={`=DATEDIF(A2, TODAY(), "Y") & " years, " & DATEDIF(A2, TODAY(), "YM") & " months"`}>
  Combines two DATEDIF calls to build a "X years, Y months" style
  tenure string.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="03" label="Project duration in total days" formula={`=DATEDIF(A2, B2, "D")`}>
  Returns the total number of days between a project's start and end
  date.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="04" label="Days until next birthday" formula={`=DATEDIF(TODAY(), DATE(YEAR(TODAY()), MONTH(A2), DAY(A2)), "D")`}>
  Rebuilds this year's version of a birth date, then measures the
  gap to today — a common trick for "days until" calculations.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="05" label="With error handling for future dates" formula={`=IFERROR(DATEDIF(A2, B2, "Y"), "Check dates")`}>
  Falls back to a message instead of `#NUM!` if start_date
  accidentally comes after end_date.
</ExampleCard>
