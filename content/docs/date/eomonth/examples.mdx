---
title: EOMONTH examples
description: Worked examples for EOMONTH — billing cutoffs, quarter-end dates, first-of-next-month, and days remaining in a month.
---

<FunctionNav />

<ExampleCard number="01" label="Basic — last day of the current month" formula={`=EOMONTH(TODAY(), 0)`}>
  Returns today's month-end date, regardless of what day of the
  month it currently is.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="02" label="First day of next month" formula={`=EOMONTH(TODAY(), 0) + 1`}>
  Adding 1 to this month's last day lands on the 1st of next month.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="03" label="Billing cutoff 3 months out" formula={`=EOMONTH(A2, 3)`}>
  Finds the last day of the month that falls 3 months after A2 — a
  common pattern for quarterly billing or renewal cutoffs.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="04" label="Days remaining in the current month" formula={`=EOMONTH(TODAY(), 0) - TODAY()`}>
  Subtracting today from this month's last day gives the number of
  days left in the month.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="05" label="Looking back to a prior month-end" formula={`=EOMONTH(A2, -1)`}>
  A negative `months` value finds the last day of the month before
  A2's month.
</ExampleCard>
