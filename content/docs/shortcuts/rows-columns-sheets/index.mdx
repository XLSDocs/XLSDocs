---
title: Rows, columns & sheets shortcuts
description: Insert, delete, hide, and manage rows, columns, and worksheets from the keyboard.
---

Restructuring a sheet — adding a row, hiding a column, inserting a
new tab — happens constantly while building a workbook. These
shortcuts cover the structural edits themselves, separate from
selecting or formatting the cells inside them.

<Callout type="idea">
  Selecting **entire rows or columns** (not just a cell range) before
  grouping skips the "Rows or Columns?" dialog entirely — the group
  applies in one keystroke. With only a cell range selected, Excel
  has to ask which direction you mean.
</Callout>
