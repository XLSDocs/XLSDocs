---
title: Formatting shortcuts
description: Bold, borders, number formats, and the Format Cells dialog, from the keyboard.
---

Most number formats and text styles have a dedicated shortcut —
faster than opening the ribbon's Home tab for something you apply
dozens of times a day.

<Callout type="idea">
  The number-format shortcuts (`Ctrl+Shift+` plus a symbol) are easy
  to forget individually, but they share a logic worth remembering
  once: `$` for Currency, `%` for Percentage, `#` for Date — the
  symbol is a visual hint for the format it applies.
</Callout>
