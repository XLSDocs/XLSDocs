---
title: Navigation shortcuts
description: Move between cells, sheets, and screens in Excel without touching the mouse.
---

Moving around a large worksheet by clicking and scrolling is slow —
these shortcuts jump directly to the edge of your data, the next
sheet, or any screen's worth of cells in one keystroke.

<Callout type="idea">
  **Ctrl+Arrow key** (⌘+Arrow key on Mac) is the single most useful
  navigation shortcut on this list — it jumps straight to the last
  cell before a gap in the current direction, the same movement
  double-clicking a cell's border performs. Tap it repeatedly to hop
  across an entire table column by column instead of scrolling.
</Callout>
