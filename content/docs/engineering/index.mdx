Engineering functions cover the specialized math that shows up in
electrical engineering, signal processing, and low-level data work —
converting between binary/octal/decimal/hexadecimal, manipulating
individual bits, doing arithmetic on complex numbers, and evaluating
a handful of named special functions (Bessel, error) that come up in
physics and statistics.
