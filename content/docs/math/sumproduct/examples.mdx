---
title: SUMPRODUCT examples
description: Worked examples for SUMPRODUCT — a weighted average, OR-logic counting, and combining with IS-functions.
---

<FunctionNav />

<ExampleCard number="01" label="Basic — sum of products" formula={`=SUMPRODUCT(Sales[Quantity], Sales[Price])`}>
  Multiplies each row's quantity by its price, then sums every
  result — a line-item total without a helper column.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="02" label="Weighted average" formula={`=SUMPRODUCT(Scores[Value], Scores[Weight]) / SUM(Scores[Weight])`}>
  Multiplies each score by its weight, sums the products, then
  divides by the total weight.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="03" label="AND logic — counting matching rows" formula={`=SUMPRODUCT((Sales[Region]="West")*(Sales[Status]="Open"))`}>
  Multiplying two boolean arrays counts rows where both conditions
  are true — equivalent to COUNTIFS with two conditions.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="04" label="OR logic — counting either condition" formula={`=SUMPRODUCT((Sales[Region]="West")+(Sales[Region]="East"))`}>
  Adding boolean arrays counts rows matching either condition —
  something COUNTIFS can't express directly.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="05" label="Counting errors with a double negative" formula={`=SUMPRODUCT(--ISERROR(Sales[Amount]))`}>
  The double negative coerces the TRUE/FALSE array from ISERROR into
  1s and 0s so SUMPRODUCT can sum it.
</ExampleCard>
