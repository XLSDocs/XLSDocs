---
title: MINIFS examples
description: Worked examples for MINIFS — basic matching, multiple conditions, and combining with MIN.
---

<FunctionNav />

<ExampleCard number="01" label="Basic — lowest price by category" formula={`=MINIFS(Products[Price], Products[Category], "Widgets")`}>
  Returns the smallest price among rows where Category equals
  "Widgets".
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="02" label="Multiple conditions" formula={`=MINIFS(Sales[Amount], Sales[Region], "West", Sales[Quarter], "Q4")`}>
  Both conditions must be true (AND logic) — West region **and** Q4.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="03" label="Earliest deadline among open projects" formula={`=MINIFS(Projects[DueDate], Projects[Status], "Open")`}>
  MINIFS works on dates too, since Excel stores them as numbers —
  this finds the nearest due date among open projects.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="04" label="OR logic via two calls" formula={`=MIN(MINIFS(Sales[Amount], Sales[Region], "West"), MINIFS(Sales[Amount], Sales[Region], "East"))`}>
  MINIFS alone only ANDs conditions — wrapping two calls in MIN
  answers "the smallest sale in West OR East."
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="05" label="Dynamic criteria from a cell" formula={`=MINIFS(Sales[Amount], Sales[Region], D2)`}>
  Referencing a cell instead of hardcoding "West" lets the same
  formula answer a different question when D2 changes.
</ExampleCard>
