Round `number` down to the nearest integer or multiple of
`significance`, always rounding toward negative infinity — negative
numbers included — regardless of the sign of either argument.

## Return value

`number` rounded down to the nearest multiple of `significance`,
always moving toward negative infinity. `FLOOR.PRECISE(-4.1)`
returns `-5`.

## Description

FLOOR.PRECISE solves the same sign headache for FLOOR that
CEILING.PRECISE solves for CEILING: instead of requiring `number`
and `significance` to share a sign, it always rounds toward negative
infinity, no matter what sign either argument has. For a negative
`number`, that means rounding *away* from zero (`-4.1` becomes
`-5`).

<ExcelCode>{`=FLOOR.PRECISE(-4.1) → -5 (toward negative infinity)`}</ExcelCode>

<Callout type="info">
  **FLOOR.PRECISE has no ISO-named counterpart.** CEILING.PRECISE
  has an equivalent, ISO.CEILING, but there's no corresponding
  ISO.FLOOR function — FLOOR.PRECISE is the only one of its kind.
</Callout>

## Common errors

### Zero result from a zero argument

<ExcelCode>{`FLOOR.PRECISE(0, 5) → 0`}</ExcelCode>
<ExcelCode>{`FLOOR.PRECISE(4.3, 0) → 0`}</ExcelCode>

If either `number` or `significance` is `0`, the result is `0` —
there's no non-zero multiple to round a zero value to.

## Related functions

- [CEILING.PRECISE](/docs/math/ceiling-precise) and
  [ISO.CEILING](/docs/math/iso-ceiling) — the rounding-up
  counterparts, always rounding toward positive infinity.
- [FLOOR.MATH](/docs/math/floor-math) — adds a `mode` argument to
  control negative-number rounding direction, rather than always
  rounding toward negative infinity.
- [FLOOR](/docs/math/floor) — the older version, requiring `number`
  and `significance` to share a sign.
