---
title: ROUND examples
description: Worked examples for ROUND — decimal places, whole numbers, and rounding to the nearest ten or hundred.
---

<FunctionNav />

<ExampleCard number="01" label="Basic — two decimal places" formula={`=ROUND(3.14159, 2)`}>
  Returns `3.14` — the two decimal digits closest to the actual
  value.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="02" label="Rounding to a whole number" formula={`=ROUND(3.6, 0)`}>
  With `num_digits` at 0, returns the nearest integer — `4`.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="03" label="Rounding to the nearest hundred" formula={`=ROUND(1250, -2)`}>
  A negative `num_digits` rounds left of the decimal point — this
  returns `1300`.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="04" label="Rounding a formula result" formula={`=ROUND(SUM(Sales[Amount]), 2)`}>
  Wrapping SUM in ROUND rounds the total itself, not just its
  display.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="05" label="Negative numbers round the same way" formula={`=ROUND(-2.5, 0)`}>
  Returns `-3` — `.5` rounds away from zero in both directions, not
  just upward.
</ExampleCard>
