---
title: ROUNDUP examples
description: Worked examples for ROUNDUP — box/shipment counting, billing periods, and negative numbers.
---

<FunctionNav />

<ExampleCard number="01" label="Basic — round up to a whole number" formula={`=ROUNDUP(3.01, 0)`}>
  Returns `4` — even though `3.01` is much closer to `3`, ROUNDUP
  always moves away from zero.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="02" label="How many boxes are needed" formula={`=ROUNDUP(Orders[Quantity]/24, 0)`}>
  Rounds up to a whole box, even if the last box is only
  partially full.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="03" label="Rounding up to two decimal places" formula={`=ROUNDUP(19.001, 2)`}>
  Returns `19.01` — any nonzero remainder past the target digit
  rounds up.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="04" label="Billing periods for a partial month" formula={`=ROUNDUP(DaysUsed/30, 0)`}>
  A single extra day still counts as a full additional billing
  period.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="05" label="Negative numbers round away from zero too" formula={`=ROUNDUP(-3.01, 0)`}>
  Returns `-4`, not `-3` — "away from zero" means more negative for
  negative inputs.
</ExampleCard>
