---
title: MEDIAN examples
description: Worked examples for MEDIAN — basic usage, an outlier comparison against AVERAGE, and grouped medians.
---

<FunctionNav />

<ExampleCard number="01" label="Basic — median salary" formula={`=MEDIAN(Salaries[Amount])`}>
  Returns the middle salary once every value is sorted.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="02" label="Median vs average with an outlier" formula={`=MEDIAN(45000, 48000, 52000, 550000)`}>
  Returns `50000` — barely moved by the one very high value, unlike
  AVERAGE of the same numbers (`173750`).
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="03" label="Median of a filtered subset" formula={`=MEDIAN(FILTER(Salaries[Amount], Salaries[Department]="Engineering"))`}>
  Filters to one department first, then finds the median within
  just those rows.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="04" label="Comparing median and average side by side" formula={`=MEDIAN(Salaries[Amount]) - AVERAGE(Salaries[Amount])`}>
  A large gap between the two is itself a signal that the data is
  skewed by outliers.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="05" label="Multiple ranges" formula={`=MEDIAN(Q1Salaries[Amount], Q2Salaries[Amount])`}>
  MEDIAN treats multiple ranges as one combined pool of numbers.
</ExampleCard>
