---
title: FLOOR examples
description: Worked examples for FLOOR — pricing to the nearest nickel, billing units, and combining with CEILING.
---

<FunctionNav />

<ExampleCard number="01" label="Basic — round down to the nearest 5" formula={`=FLOOR(22, 5)`}>
  Returns `20` — the previous multiple of 5 at or below 22.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="02" label="Pricing to the nearest nickel" formula={`=FLOOR(Prices[Amount], 0.05)`}>
  Rounds every price down to the previous 5-cent increment.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="03" label="Whole billing units only" formula={`=FLOOR(Usage[Minutes], 15)`}>
  Rounds usage down to the last complete 15-minute billing unit,
  ignoring a partial unit at the end.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="04" label="Negative numbers need a matching sign" formula={`=FLOOR(-22, -5)`}>
  Returns `-25` — both arguments need to be negative together, or
  the formula returns `#NUM!`.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="05" label="Comparing FLOOR and CEILING" formula={`=CEILING(A2, 5) - FLOOR(A2, 5)`}>
  Together, FLOOR and CEILING bracket a value between the nearest
  multiples below and above it — this returns the width of that
  bracket.
</ExampleCard>
