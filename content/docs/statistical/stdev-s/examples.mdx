---
title: STDEV.S examples
description: Worked examples for STDEV.S — test score spread, quality control, and comparing to STDEV.P.
---

<FunctionNav />

<ExampleCard number="01" label="Basic — spread of test scores" formula={`=STDEV.S(Scores[Value])`}>
  Returns the sample standard deviation of a set of test scores.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="02" label="Quality control tolerance" formula={`=STDEV.S(Batch[Weight]) * 3`}>
  A common manufacturing check — three standard deviations from the
  mean, used to flag statistically unusual measurements.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="03" label="Comparing sample vs population" formula={`=STDEV.S(Data[Value]) - STDEV.P(Data[Value])`}>
  Shows the (usually small) gap between the sample and population
  calculations on the same data — always non-negative.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="04" label="Coefficient of variation" formula={`=STDEV.S(Prices[Amount]) / AVERAGE(Prices[Amount])`}>
  Standard deviation relative to the mean — useful for comparing
  spread across datasets with very different scales.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="05" label="Flagging outliers beyond 2 standard deviations" formula={`=ABS(A2 - AVERAGE(Data[Value])) > 2 * STDEV.S(Data[Value])`}>
  Flags a value as unusual if it's more than two standard deviations
  from the mean.
</ExampleCard>
