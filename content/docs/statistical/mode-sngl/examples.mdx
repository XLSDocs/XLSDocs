---
title: MODE.SNGL examples
description: Worked examples for MODE.SNGL — most common order size, handling ties, and comparing to MODE.MULT.
---

<FunctionNav />

<ExampleCard number="01" label="Basic — most common order quantity" formula={`=MODE.SNGL(Orders[Quantity])`}>
  Returns whichever quantity appears most often across all orders.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="02" label="Most common survey rating" formula={`=MODE.SNGL(Survey[Rating])`}>
  Finds the single most frequently chosen rating value.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="03" label="Handling the no-repeat case" formula={`=IFERROR(MODE.SNGL(Prices[Amount]), "No repeated value")`}>
  Returns a friendly message instead of `#N/A` when every price in
  the range happens to be unique.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="04" label="Checking whether the mode is actually a tie" formula={`=COUNTA(MODE.MULT(Orders[Quantity])) > 1`}>
  Compares MODE.SNGL's single answer against MODE.MULT's full spilled
  list to check whether more than one value was actually tied for
  most common.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="05" label="Mode within a filtered subset" formula={`=MODE.SNGL(FILTER(Orders[Quantity], Orders[Region]="West"))`}>
  Filters to West-region orders first, then finds the most common
  quantity within just those rows.
</ExampleCard>
