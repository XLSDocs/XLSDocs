---
title: RANK.EQ examples
description: Worked examples for RANK.EQ — sales leaderboards, ascending order, and tie-breaking with COUNTIF.
---

<FunctionNav />

<ExampleCard number="01" label="Basic — sales leaderboard" formula={`=RANK.EQ(A2, $B$2:$B$10)`}>
  Ranks each salesperson's total against the whole team, largest
  first.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="02" label="Ascending order — fastest time wins" formula={`=RANK.EQ(A2, $B$2:$B$10, 1)`}>
  Ranks smallest first, useful for race times or any "lower is
  better" metric.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="03" label="Breaking ties into unique ranks" formula={`=RANK.EQ(A2,$B$2:$B$10) + COUNTIF($B$2:A2,A2) - 1`}>
  Nudges each tied duplicate to a distinct sequential rank instead of
  sharing one.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="04" label="Top 3 flag" formula={`=RANK.EQ(A2, $B$2:$B$10) <= 3`}>
  Flags whether a row is in the top 3 by rank.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="05" label="Rank within a filtered group" formula={`=RANK.EQ(A2, FILTER($B$2:$B$20, $C$2:$C$20=C2))`}>
  Ranks a value only against rows in its own group (e.g. its own
  region), instead of the whole list.
</ExampleCard>
