---
title: MsgBox examples
description: Worked examples for MsgBox — simple notifications, Yes/No confirmations, and multi-line messages.
---

<FunctionNav />

<ExampleCard number="01" label="Basic — simple notification" formula={`MsgBox "Import complete."`}>
  Shows a plain message with a single OK button, no return value
  captured.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="02" label="Yes/No confirmation" formula={`If MsgBox("Overwrite existing file?", vbYesNo) = vbYes Then`}>
  Captures the result and branches on whether the user clicked Yes
  or No.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="03" label="Custom title and icon" formula={`MsgBox "Invalid entry.", vbCritical, "Input Error"`}>
  Sets a custom title bar and shows a red critical-error icon instead
  of the default.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="04" label="Multi-line message" formula={`MsgBox "Row " & i & " skipped:" & vbCrLf & "Missing required field"`}>
  Uses vbCrLf to break the message onto a second line.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="05" label="Three-way Yes/No/Cancel" formula={`result = MsgBox("Save changes before closing?", vbYesNoCancel)`}>
  Captures one of three possible outcomes — vbYes, vbNo, or
  vbCancel — for a three-way branch.
</ExampleCard>
