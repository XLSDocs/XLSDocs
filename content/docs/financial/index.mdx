---
title: Financial
description: Calculate loan payments, present and future value, and rates of return.
---

Financial functions handle the time-value-of-money math behind loans,
investments, and cash flow analysis — payments on a mortgage, what a
future sum is worth today, or the rate of return on a series of
uneven cash flows.
