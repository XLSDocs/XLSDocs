description: Worked examples for PMT — a standard mortgage payment, an auto loan, and the sign-convention and rate-conversion gotchas.

<FunctionNav />

<ExampleCard number="01" label="30-year mortgage payment" formula={`=-PMT(5%/12, 30*12, 200000)`}>
  A $200,000 loan at 5% annual interest, paid monthly over 30 years —
  the annual rate is divided by 12 and the term multiplied by 12 to
  match the monthly payment period.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="02" label="5-year auto loan" formula={`=-PMT(6.5%/12, 5*12, 28000)`}>
  Same shape, shorter term — a $28,000 loan at 6.5% annual interest
  over 5 years of monthly payments.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="03" label="Without negating the result" formula={`=PMT(5%/12, 30*12, 200000)`}>
  Returns `-1073.64` — the raw sign convention, useful when the
  payment feeds into another calculation that expects a negative
  (outflow) value.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="04" label="Payments due at the start of the period" formula={`=-PMT(5%/12, 30*12, 200000, 0, 1)`}>
  Setting `type` to `1` calculates a slightly lower payment, since
  each payment has one fewer period to accrue interest.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="05" label="Saving toward a target instead of paying off a loan" formula={`=-PMT(4%/12, 10*12, 0, 50000)`}>
  With `pv` at 0 and `fv` set to a target, PMT instead calculates
  the monthly contribution needed to reach $50,000 in 10 years.
</ExampleCard>
