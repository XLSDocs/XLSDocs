title: IPMT examples
description: Worked examples for IPMT — the first and last payment of a loan, and confirming it matches PMT and PPMT.
---

<FunctionNav />

<ExampleCard number="01" label="Interest portion of the first payment" formula={`=IPMT(5%/12, 1, 30*12, 200000)`}>
  On a $200,000 loan at 5% over 30 years, about $833.33 of the first
  ~$1,073.64 payment is interest — the largest interest charge of any
  payment in the loan.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="02" label="Interest portion of the last payment" formula={`=IPMT(5%/12, 360, 30*12, 200000)`}>
  By the final payment (period 360), the interest portion has shrunk
  to almost nothing — the balance being charged interest on is nearly
  zero by then.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="03" label="Confirming IPMT + PPMT equals PMT" formula={`=IPMT(5%/12,1,360,200000) + PPMT(5%/12,1,360,200000)`}>
  Adding the interest and principal portions of the same payment
  returns exactly the total payment PMT would calculate on its own.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="04" label="Building one row of an amortization schedule" formula={`=IPMT($B$1/12, ROW()-1, $B$2*12, $B$3)`}>
  Using absolute references for the loan terms and `ROW()-1` for
  `per` lets this formula be dragged down a column to generate the
  interest portion of every payment.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="05" label="Invalid payment number" formula={`=IPMT(5%/12, 400, 360, 200000)`}>
  Returns `#NUM!` — period 400 doesn't exist on a 360-payment loan;
  `per` must be between 1 and `nper`.
</ExampleCard>
