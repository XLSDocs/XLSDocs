---
title: AND examples
description: Worked examples for AND — combined with IF, multiple conditions, and the array-formula gotcha.
---

<FunctionNav />

<ExampleCard number="01" label="Basic — two conditions" formula={`=AND(B2>0, C2="Approved")`}>
  Returns `TRUE` only if B2 is positive **and** C2 equals "Approved".
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="02" label="Nested inside IF" formula={`=IF(AND(B2>0, C2="Approved"), "Ship", "Hold")`}>
  The most common real-world pattern — branch on the combined result
  of two or more conditions.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="03" label="More than two conditions" formula={`=AND(B2>0, C2="Approved", D2<=100)`}>
  AND accepts up to 254 conditions — every one of them must be TRUE.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="04" label="Combined with OR" formula={`=IF(AND(B2>0, OR(C2="Approved", C2="Pending")), "Process", "Hold")`}>
  Nesting AND and OR together checks "B2 is positive AND (status is
  Approved OR Pending)."
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="05" label="Row-by-row inside FILTER" formula={`=FILTER(A2:C10, (B2:B10="West")*(C2:C10="Q4"))`}>
  AND won't work row-by-row inside FILTER — multiply the boolean
  arrays together instead.
</ExampleCard>
