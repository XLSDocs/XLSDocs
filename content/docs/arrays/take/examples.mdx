---
title: TAKE examples
description: Worked examples for TAKE — the first and last rows, combining row and column limits, and the direction-sign gotcha.
---

<FunctionNav />

<ExampleCard number="01" label="First 10 rows" formula={`=TAKE(A1:C1000, 10)`}>
  Returns just the first 10 rows across all 3 columns, regardless of
  how many rows the source range actually has.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="02" label="Last 10 rows" formula={`=TAKE(A1:C1000, -10)`}>
  The negative sign flips the direction — this pulls the 10 most
  recent rows instead of the first 10.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="03" label="First 3 columns only" formula={`=TAKE(A1:F100, 100, 3)`}>
  Keeps every row but limits the result to just the first 3 columns
  of a 6-column range.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="04" label="Last 5 rows of the last 2 columns" formula={`=TAKE(A1:F100, -5, -2)`}>
  Combining two negative arguments pulls from the bottom-right
  corner of the range — the last 5 rows of the last 2 columns.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="05" label="A live 'most recent orders' snapshot" formula={`=SORT(TAKE(SORT(A2:C500, 3, -1), 5), 3, -1)`}>
  Sorts by date descending, takes the top 5 (most recent), then
  re-sorts them back into chronological order for display.
</ExampleCard>
