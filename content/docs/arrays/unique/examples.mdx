---
title: UNIQUE examples
description: Worked examples for UNIQUE — basic distinct lists, exactly-once filtering, and combining with SORT and FILTER.
---

<FunctionNav />

<ExampleCard number="01" label="Basic — distinct list" formula={`=UNIQUE(Sales[Region])`}>
  Returns every distinct region, each shown once, in order of first
  appearance.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="02" label="Sorted distinct list" formula={`=SORT(UNIQUE(Sales[Region]))`}>
  Wrapping UNIQUE in SORT alphabetizes the distinct list instead of
  leaving it in first-appearance order.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="03" label="Values appearing exactly once" formula={`=UNIQUE(Sales[Region], FALSE, TRUE)`}>
  With `exactly_once=TRUE`, only regions that appear **exactly one
  time** in the data are returned — duplicated regions are excluded
  entirely, not collapsed to one row.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="04" label="Distinct values matching a condition" formula={`=UNIQUE(FILTER(Sales[Region], Sales[Status]="Open"))`}>
  Filters to open orders first, then returns the distinct regions
  among just those rows.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="05" label="Counting distinct values" formula={`=ROWS(UNIQUE(Sales[Region]))`}>
  Wrapping UNIQUE in ROWS returns how many distinct regions exist,
  as a single number instead of a spilled list.
</ExampleCard>
