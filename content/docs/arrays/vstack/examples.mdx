---
title: VSTACK examples
description: Worked examples for VSTACK — combining monthly sheets, appending new records, and deduplicating with UNIQUE.
---

<FunctionNav />

<ExampleCard number="01" label="Basic — combine two ranges" formula={`=VSTACK(A1:C5, D1:F5)`}>
  Stacks the second range directly below the first.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="02" label="Combining monthly sheets" formula={`=VSTACK(Jan!A1:C10, Feb!A1:C10, Mar!A1:C10)`}>
  Merges three separate sheets into one continuous list.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="03" label="Appending new records to an existing table" formula={`=VSTACK(Sales[#All], NewRecords[#All])`}>
  Combines an existing table with a staging range of new rows,
  headers included.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="04" label="Deduplicating a combined list" formula={`=UNIQUE(VSTACK(ListA, ListB))`}>
  Combines two lists and removes duplicate rows in one formula.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="05" label="Sorting a combined list" formula={`=SORT(VSTACK(RegionA, RegionB))`}>
  Combines two ranges and sorts the merged result.
</ExampleCard>
