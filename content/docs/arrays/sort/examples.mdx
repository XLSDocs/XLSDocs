---
title: SORT examples
description: Worked examples for SORT — ascending and descending order, sorting by a specific column, and combining with FILTER and UNIQUE.
---

<FunctionNav />

<ExampleCard number="01" label="Basic — ascending by the first column" formula={`=SORT(Sales[#All])`}>
  Sorts every row by the first column, ascending — the default when
  no other arguments are given.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="02" label="Descending order" formula={`=SORT(Sales[#All], 1, -1)`}>
  Passing `-1` as `sort_order` reverses the sort to descending.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="03" label="Sort by a specific column" formula={`=SORT(Sales[#All], 3, -1)`}>
  `sort_index` picks which column to sort by — here, the 3rd column,
  largest first.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="04" label="Combined with FILTER" formula={`=SORT(FILTER(Sales[#All], Sales[Region]="West"), 3, -1)`}>
  Filters to West-region rows first, then sorts the result by the
  3rd column, largest first.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="05" label="Combined with UNIQUE" formula={`=SORT(UNIQUE(Sales[Region]))`}>
  Produces an alphabetized list of every distinct region — UNIQUE
  removes duplicates, SORT alphabetizes what's left.
</ExampleCard>
