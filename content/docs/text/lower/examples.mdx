---
title: LOWER examples
description: Worked examples for LOWER — normalizing emails, case-insensitive comparisons, and combining with TRIM.
---

<FunctionNav />

<ExampleCard number="01" label="Basic — normalize an email address" formula={`=LOWER(A2)`}>
  Converts "Jane.Doe@EXAMPLE.com" to "jane.doe@example.com".
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="02" label="Case-insensitive comparison" formula={`=IF(LOWER(A2)=LOWER(B2), "Match", "No match")`}>
  Lowercasing both sides before comparing avoids false mismatches
  caused only by casing differences.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="03" label="Cleaning up spacing and casing together" formula={`=TRIM(LOWER(A2))`}>
  Removes extra spaces and normalizes casing in one formula.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="04" label="Building a consistent username" formula={`=LOWER(SUBSTITUTE(A2, " ", "."))`}>
  Combines LOWER with SUBSTITUTE to turn "Jane Doe" into
  "jane.doe" — a common username-generation pattern.
</ExampleCard>

<ExampleCard number="05" label="Making a permanent change" formula={`=LOWER(A2:A10) then Paste Special > Values`}>
  Build the lowercase versions in a helper column, then paste the
  results back as values to make the change stick without keeping
  the formula.
</ExampleCard>
